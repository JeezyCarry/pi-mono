**An authorized danger now asks you instead of denying outright.** When the anchor names the flagged action, or the reviewer is unsure the category applies, the classifier confirms with you (naming the category) rather than denying. A hit nobody asked for is still a hard deny, and the classifier never allows a danger hit — the confirmation is yours to settle. The risk lane follows the same shape: a costly action the anchor asks for confirms the price instead of denying it.

In permissive mode a danger confirmation auto-allows (that mode's ladder accepts defer-danger). That is the mode's contract, not a reviewer judgment: strict still denies, and every other mode asks.
