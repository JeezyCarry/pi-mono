**The generation-time AA policy moved into its own module, and its tiers became data.** Which pi surfaces a token is painted on, and what contrast that pairing must clear, now live in one table in `aa-policy.ts`; the converter hands it the surfaces it resolved and reports what it flags. A slot's rule is readable at a glance instead of emerging from five positional arguments at fifteen call sites, and adding a slot means adding a row — a slot adjusted without one is now a compile error rather than a silent skip of enforcement.

**The contrast math moved to `core/color.ts`**, the module the glossary already names for color math, so generation and render call the same functions from one seam instead of reaching through the syntax theme. `syntax-theme.ts` is left owning the token theme alone.

The move changed no generated byte and no diagnostic line. The policy's three tiers, the style zone, the plateau between the endpoints and the unreachable-pairing case now also have rule-level tests of their own, each checked against an independent enumeration of the ramp, so a policy change fails as a broken rule instead of churning golden hex.
